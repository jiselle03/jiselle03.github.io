## Design Context

### Users
Hiring managers, engineering leads, recruiters, and peers evaluating Jiselle Liu as a fullstack engineer with a strong frontend focus. They need to understand technical range, product judgment, interface craft, and proof of recent product work quickly.

### Brand Personality
Direct, capable, front-end-forward. The portfolio should feel memorable and intentionally designed without reading as gimmicky, over-animated, or generated from a generic template.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a polished engineering portfolio direction: editorial structure, crisp typography, restrained motion, and selective visual details that show frontend taste. Recent work should be framed in NDA-safe terms around a tutoring scheduling/operations platform, an AI hiring/job-search platform, and Hum, a personal fitness/recovery tracker. The recent-work positioning should communicate that Jiselle owned product direction and shipped the build. Forward engineering can be presented as a capability: helping companies streamline workflows with pragmatic AI, without implying a specific recent engagement. The preferred current stack is TypeScript, Next.js, React, tRPC, Drizzle, PostgreSQL, Tailwind, OpenAI, Radix UI, and Vitest. Older/secondary experience can mention Claude Code, Anthropic, Vue, Ruby on Rails, Go, Express, MongoDB, React Query, and Playwright without positioning them as the primary stack.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with proof of work: project outcomes, technical decisions, and role clarity should be visible in the first meaningful viewport.
2. Keep the runtime lean: ship mostly static content with minimal client JavaScript unless an interaction clearly improves the experience.
3. Make the craft visible: typography, layout, responsiveness, focus states, and micro-interactions should demonstrate frontend judgment.
4. Avoid portfolio cliches: no generic card grid, purple gradients, decorative metrics, or vague "passionate developer" copy.
5. Preserve accessibility as part of the brand: semantic HTML, keyboard support, contrast, reduced motion, and resilient mobile layouts are non-negotiable.
